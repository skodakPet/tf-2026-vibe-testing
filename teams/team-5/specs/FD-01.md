# FD-01 · Browse restaurants
Seed: none — each test starts with page.goto('/')

UI names seen in the live app (names only, not expectations):
- Section heading: `Popular Restaurants` (h2), subtitle paragraph right below it
  (e.g. "Showing restaurants for New York, NY — 1 don't deliver there").
- Button: `View All` (next to the section heading).
- Each card is a link named after the restaurant, `href` = `/restaurant/<id>`
  (`/restaurant/1` Burger Palace, `/restaurant/4` Mediterranean Delight, `/restaurant/2` Pizza Corner,
  `/restaurant/3` Sushi Masters, `/restaurant/koliba-u-jana` Koliba u Jána).
- Inside a card: h3 with the name, paragraph with cuisines ("American, Burgers"), rating ("4.8"),
  time range ("25-35 min"), fee ("$2.99" or "Free"), optional label over the image
  (promotion, or the `Not available at your address` badge).
- Restaurant page: h1 with the restaurant name.
- Address button in the navbar: `New York, NY`.

## FD-01.1 · Card shows name, cuisines, rating, delivery time range, delivery fee (or Free)
Spec quote: "Each restaurant card shows the name, the cuisines, the rating, the delivery time range and the delivery fee (or **Free**)."
Steps:
1. Go to `/`.
2. Find the `Popular Restaurants` section and all restaurant cards in it (links to `/restaurant/...`).
3. For every card check that it contains:
   - a name heading (h3, not empty),
   - a cuisines text (one or more cuisines, separated by commas),
   - a rating (a number such as `4.8`),
   - a delivery time range (pattern `<n>-<m> min`),
   - a delivery fee: either a price (`$<n>.<nn>`) or the word `Free`.
4. Check that at least one card shows `Free` (`Pizza Corner`) and at least one shows a price (`Burger Palace`), so the test covers both fee forms.
Expected (from spec): Every card shows the name, cuisines, rating, delivery time range and delivery fee. A free delivery is shown as **Free**.

## FD-01.2 · Card shows current promotion when it has one
Spec quote: "A card shows the restaurant's current promotion when it has one — for example *20% OFF orders over $25*."
Steps:
1. Go to `/`.
2. Find the `Burger Palace` card.
3. Check that the card contains the text `20% OFF orders over $25`.
4. Find the card of a restaurant without a promotion (`Pizza Corner`) and check that it shows no promotion text. Only the rating, name, cuisines, time and fee should be there.
Expected (from spec): A restaurant with a current promotion shows it on its card (example: *20% OFF orders over $25*). Promotions appear only "when it has one".
Note: `Mediterranean Delight` also shows a promotion (`Free dessert with orders over $30`). The spec does not name it, so the test must not depend on it. Its text can be used as a second, optional check.

## FD-01.3 · Selecting a card opens the restaurant's page
Spec quote: "Selecting a card opens that restaurant's page."
Steps:
1. Go to `/`.
2. Click the `Burger Palace` card.
3. Check the URL is `/restaurant/1`.
4. Check the page heading (h1) is `Burger Palace`.
Expected (from spec): Selecting a card opens the page of the restaurant that was selected, not another one.

## FD-01.4 · View All shows the full list of restaurants
Spec quote: "**View All** shows the full list of restaurants."
Steps:
1. Go to `/`.
2. Write down the restaurant cards shown under `Popular Restaurants` (names and count).
3. Click the `View All` button.
4. Collect the restaurant cards shown now.
5. Check that the list contains every restaurant from step 2 and is the full list: the count is equal to or greater than in step 2, and no restaurant from step 2 is missing.
Expected (from spec): After **View All**, the full list of restaurants is shown.
App shows: clicking `View All` keeps the URL `/` and shows the same 5 cards as before, with no visible change. The spec does not say how many restaurants the full list has. A test can only check that no restaurant is lost. It cannot prove that more restaurants should appear.

## FD-01.5 · Non-delivering restaurant greyed out, *Not available at your address* badge, counted in subtitle, cannot be opened
Spec quote: "A restaurant that does not deliver to the current address is shown greyed out with a *Not available at your address* badge, and the subtitle counts them (*1 don't deliver there*). It cannot be opened."
Steps:
1. Go to `/` (default address `New York, NY`).
2. Find the card with the badge text `Not available at your address` (currently `Koliba u Jána`).
3. Check that the card is greyed out (for example a grayscale filter or lower opacity on the card or its image), and that delivering cards (for example `Burger Palace`) are not.
4. Check that the subtitle under `Popular Restaurants` contains `1 don't deliver there`, and that this number equals the number of cards with the badge.
5. Click the non-delivering card.
6. Check that the URL is still `/` and that no restaurant page (h1 `Koliba u Jána`) opened.
Expected (from spec): The non-delivering restaurant is greyed out and carries the *Not available at your address* badge. The subtitle counts it (*1 don't deliver there*). The card cannot be opened.
App shows: the card is a normal link to `/restaurant/koliba-u-jana`. Clicking it opens the restaurant page (h1 `Koliba u Jána`). The badge, the subtitle count and the greyed-out image (grayscale(1), opacity 0.6) are present.
